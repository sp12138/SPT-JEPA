Fit each linear/RBF readout **once on actual training observation encodings**, with1,024 frames and no validation fitting. Preserve per-coordinate train mean/std (floor1e-12), ridge and kernel rules exactly. Physical labels are `privileged_block_0_pos` and wxyz `privileged_block_0_quat`; the tensor81 target is sign-invariant and invariant to all24 proper cube symmetries. Predict the same84 target coordinates (tensor81+xyz), then report xyz and orientation separately. The quaternion decoder uses only the same1,024 training quaternions; retain the constant-training-mean and training-codebook quantization references. No pose-label variant search or extra regularization tuning.

Apply the fixed readout to the same448 validation future frames under observed-future, clean causal rollout, teacher-forced one-step, initial carry and shifted-observed carry. Primary physical reports: xyz error in cm, fraction within3cm, cube-symmetry angle and fraction within15°, and the joint readout threshold. This joint fraction is neither actual planning success nor three-physical-step hold: the saved frames are five steps apart, and the goal-dependent hold state is not labeled.

The informative comparison is observation accessibility versus forecast accessibility under the **same fitted readout**. Also retain the discrepancy between decoded forecast and decoded actual observation, rather than interpreting `forecast error − observed error` as pure dynamics error. For each Euclidean target block and fixed readout f, the exact identity is

`MSE(f(z_pred), y) = MSE(f(z_obs), y) + MSE(f(z_pred), f(z_obs)) + (2/d) E[(f(z_obs)−y)·(f(z_pred)−f(z_obs))]`, where d is the target block dimension and MSE averages coordinates.

The cross term can be negative; angular/threshold metrics have no analogous additive split. This is a descriptive decomposition, not causal mediation or a proof that all task information is absent/present. The six models have different latent dimensions/capacities (native192/18.0M, TF64/51.1M); compare physical-unit readout errors, never raw latent MSE across families. Probe capacity, finite training samples and correlated within-clip frames remain limitations:1,024 frames are128 episodes. No performance conclusion follows before the actual fixed readout results.
