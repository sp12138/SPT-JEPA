This uses additional executed fork data from50 **validation** episodes, so none of its scores are original-setting benchmark results or deployable world-model improvements. No encoder/dynamics training, GPU, simulation, new cases, physical-label fitting, tuning, early stopping, or automatic continuation.

Inputs are only the existing TF12000 seed1/2 saved initial64D states, true39×64D endpoints and39×25×5 raw action sequences. Sort the50 unique source episode IDs and alternate assignment to folds0/1. Both model seeds and all39 branches from an episode share a fold. For each heldout fold, train on the other25 episodes. The dataset's actual evaluation goals and all physical/success fields are excluded from the fitting data structure.

One scalar compatibility head `MLP(253,192,192,1)` with GELU after the two hidden layers reads `[initial/RMS, (goal−initial)/RMS, standardized_raw_actions.flatten()]`. It sees one candidate at a time, with no bank index, episode ID, whole-bank features or batch negatives. Normalize actions using the checkpoint config's dataset-card training mean/std in the original float32 subtract/divide order. The single RMS is `sqrt(mean(z²))` over **all** true endpoint and initial coordinates of the25 training episodes, without centering; zero or nonfinite fails. No per-coordinate normalization.

AdamW lr1e−3, weight_decay.01, standard betas/epsilon; no schedule, clipping, augmentation or stopping rule. CPU2 threads. Seed20261101+trainingseed for initialization and sampling, reset identically for both folds. Run seed1/fold0, seed1/fold1, seed2/fold0, seed2/fold1 sequentially. A global15-minute wall-time ceiling preserves the current partial state and aborts; no extra updates are allowed.

Evaluate the heldout head on the original actual goal and same39 candidates, with22 nonoracle candidates as primary and all39 as appendix.
