No GPU, encoder/model forward, simulator, training, projection fitting, matrix selection or checkpoint modification. Existing Cube fullpatch arrays only: `reports/carrier-spatial600-20261031/seed{1,2}-features/{train,validation}__fullpatch__full.npy`, with original128×8 training and64×8 validation frames. Reuse the same1024 train /448 validation future frames for the original fixed readout protocol. Both seeds and both pre-existing readouts remain; no validation selection.

For one2×2 cell, u=(p00,p01,p10,p11)∈R^768. Old A(u)=((p00+p01+p10+p11)/4)P, P∈R^(192×16), exact original `SeedSequence([20261030,192,16])` Gaussian divided by4. One new map only: B(u)=uG/2, G∈R^(768×16) Gaussian divided by4, fixed `SeedSequence([20261030,768,16])`. The single global1/2 factor compares average patch energy and matches expected gain on four-identical-patch directions; it is not a tuned mixing coefficient or another arm. It also cancels under the production isotropic standardization and diagnostic coordinate standardization.

The same map is shared across all64 addressed cells. Within-cell order is (dy,dx,channel), with dy/dx row-major. Both measurements have64×16=1024 outputs and rank16 per cell; total nullity remains752. Old additionally annihilates the entire576-D block-zero-sum subspace. New has no predetermined zero-sum blind subspace, but cannot preserve every direction at width16. For fixed u and random matrices, E||A(u)||²=||mean(u)||² and E||B(u)||²=||u||²/4. Decomposing u=P_DC u+u_perp gives E||B(u)||²−E||A(u)||²=||u_perp||²/4. The fixed realization need not satisfy equality. For a fixed nonzero cell direction, normalized new gain is chi-square16/16, with relative standard deviation sqrt(2/16); this is not a uniform JL guarantee over all768 directions.
