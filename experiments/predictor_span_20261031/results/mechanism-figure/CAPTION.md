Prediction error decomposition at completed checkpoints, alongside planning.** Panels A–B split clean causal-rollout latent MSE into the oracle error outside the current predictor's affine output space (orange) and the residual within that space (blue). All six checkpoints per task, including both training seeds, use 1,024-dimensional observation codes. Local 2k and 6k observation codes are identical; basis 2k uses a fixed orthogonal rotation, preserving Euclidean scale (observed maximum rotation discrepancy <2.4×10⁻⁷). Compare MSE within each task; no 64D model or unrelated latent scale is included. Each bar averages seven forecast horizons over the same 64 held-out windows (448 targets). End labels give total MSE and the outside-space fraction. Panel C gives each corresponding checkpoint's success rate on the original primary and fresh sets of 50 cases, with the original-command controller. These are separate case sets; percentages are descriptive and include no uncertainty estimate for environment repeat variability.

For the frozen final LayerNorm→linear predictor output, let V = W diag(γ)(I−11ᵀ/192), c = Wβ+b, and P be the orthogonal projector onto range(V). The k-step forecast lies in u₀+kc+range(V). Its current-weight oracle MSE is ‖(I−P)(uₖ−u₀−kc)‖²/1,024. All twelve effective spaces have rank 191, and the measured total = outside + inside decomposition has relative error <3×10⁻⁹. The bound allows arbitrary hidden vectors; action conditioning, attention and LayerNorm's radius may further constrain attainable forecasts. It is a bound for these frozen weights, not an architectural impossibility or evidence that training cannot rotate the output space.

Basis 2k has lower total MSE but a higher outside-space oracle than local 2k in both tasks and both seeds. Improved fitting inside the reachable output space can therefore coexist with poorer coverage of the observed future displacement. This is a geometric diagnostic, not a causal explanation of planning: the inaccessible component need not be task-relevant, and the outside component of a fixed-goal cost is action-independent at a fixed horizon. Differences between 2k and 6k are categorical checkpoint comparisons, not an identified causal training trend. No new model forward pass, GPU run, physical-label fit or evaluation was performed to make this figure.
